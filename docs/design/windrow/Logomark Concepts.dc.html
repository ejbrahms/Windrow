Windrow logomark — 3 directions

A "more minimal, no colors" round briefly redrew 1a as flat monochrome — that feedback was meant for the site's chrome, not this mark. Retracted; the logo is back to its original pick below, unchanged.

A windrow is the raked row of material left behind a pass across a field — the trail, not the moving air. Each direction below is built to read as a row/trail at 24px nav size, shown against the dark chrome from the existing redesign spec.

1a — Raked rows (tapering wedges)
Three parallel diagonal strokes, wide where the rake starts, tapering to a fine point where each row's material runs out. Decreasing width top→bottom reads as the pass thinning out.

picked — unchanged
1b — Furrow curl (rows piling to one end)
Three parallel strokes at uneven start points (like rake tines), all folding into one shared curl where the material piles at the row's end.
Windrow
1c — Wake chevrons (receding trail)
Three chevrons of decreasing size trailing off to the left, like footprints or a wake receding behind a pass. Reads more generically as "path/progress" than the other two.
Windrow

Pick: 1a — most literal "windrow" (rows tapering off where material runs out, diagonal to imply the pass direction), cleanest silhouette at 24px, and the only one of the three with no closed loops/curls that could still read as a breeze icon.
